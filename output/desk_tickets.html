Who the Boss calls first, and why

Inventory
A customer order is a stock question before it is anything else. Whether Tauhid gets a tee today or has to wait depends on how many S tees are on the shelf, so the Boss sends this to Inventory first. The Boss's own get_ticket shows the S row at qty 0 and invoice 501 linked, but only Inventory can tell whether a restock is possible.
